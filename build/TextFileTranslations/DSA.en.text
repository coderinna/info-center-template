# Digital Services Act (DSA) and how it affects us
The European Union’s new Digital Services Act (DSA) will come into force in August 2024. It is designed to make the web a safer and more responsible place for all users.

# What does DSA mean for you?

A safer platform: We must ensure that there is no illegal or harmful content on our service. If any content breaks the rules, we will act quickly and remove it.

Your rights are preserved: You have the right to know why content has been removed and you can appeal the decision.

Transparency: We regularly publish reports on how we manage content and advertising on our platform.

Responsible operations: As a large platform, we follow strict requirements to ensure that info_center_template is trustworthy and fair for everyone.

# Why is DSA important?

DSA helps keep the web safe without you having to worry about harmful content or being treated unfairly. It also gives you more control over what happens on the platform.

At info_center_template, we are committed to
Complying with the DSA in full.

Respecting your privacy and keeping your information secure.

Giving you a safe and open environment to be yourself.
