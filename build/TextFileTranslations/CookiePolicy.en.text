# What are cookies?
A cookie (or more familiarly "cookie") is a file stored on your computer,
which is stored when you visit websites.

The file contains a small amount of information in text format about what you do on the website.

We primarily use cookies to identify you when you log in to the service.
**Using the service without cookies while logged in would therefore not be possible.**

In addition, cookies are used **for advertising targeting, user research and user measurement.**

We cannot use cookies to identify who has visited the site, but we can use cookies to improve the user experience of the site.

# Are cookies harmful?

Cookies do not cause harm to your computer or internet use.

Cookies are small text files that do not contain viruses. In addition, they do not load your computer's resources such as memory.

# How can I prevent cookies from being stored on my computer?
You can prevent cookies from being stored in your browser settings.

Please note that blocking cookies may prevent you from using info_center_template while logged in.

# How can I delete cookies?
You can delete cookies in your browser settings. This varies depending on the type and version of your browser.

Please note that after deleting cookies, you will be automatically logged out.

For more information, please refer to your browser's instructions.

Please note that you will have to log in to info_center_template again after clearing your cookies, as your identification information will no longer be valid.
