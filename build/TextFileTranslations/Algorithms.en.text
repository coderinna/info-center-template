# Recommendations and content customization in our service
We use recommendations and targeted advertising in our service based on user data,
to provide users with more interesting and relevant content. Your user experience is important to us, and we strive to provide content and ads
that better match your interests and usage habits.

# What does this mean for you?
##Personalized recommendations:
On the homepage and other content, you may see videos and content based on your viewing history, interests, and use of the service.

##Targeted advertising:
We may use user data, such as browsing behavior, location, and previous use, to show you the most relevant ads.

##Privacy and security:
We process user data in accordance with data protection legislation and ensure the secure processing of data.

##Clear content selection:
We offer content that is both popular and as relevant to the user as possible, utilizing personalization.

# Ads and Cookies
We use cookies and other technologies to improve your experience, personalize content
and display targeted advertising. Advertising may be based on, for example, your activity on the service, browsing history, location and other user data.

You can control the use of cookies and advertising targeting in the cookie settings found at the bottom of the website.
