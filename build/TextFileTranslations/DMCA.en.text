# DMCA – Copyright Notices and Content Removal Requests
info_center_template respects copyrights and will promptly respond to notices of copyright infringement in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and other applicable laws.

# How to File a Copyright Notice?
If you believe that material on info_center_template's service infringes your copyright, you may submit a notice of copyright infringement as follows:

Use our DMCA notification form at: https://www.info_center_template.com/info under DMCA -> Report DMCA

Notifications must be made by the copyright owner or an authorized agent.

The notification must include the following information:

- Identification of the copyrighted work or a representative list of such works
- The exact location on the site of the infringing material (e.g., media URL)
- Contact information for the notifier
- A good faith statement that the material is not authorized to be used
- A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or an authorized agent
- Signature (may be electronic, e.g., a typed name)

# Counter-Notifications
If you are the uploader of the material and believe that the content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification.

- The counter-notification must include an explanation of why the content you uploaded was removed in error.

- A statement of the accuracy of the information in good faith that the content was removed in error.

# Repeat Infringers
info_center_template has a zero tolerance policy for repeat copyright infringers.

Accounts of repeat infringers may be terminated or blocked without prior notice.
